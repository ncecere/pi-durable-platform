# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## In short

- Be welcoming, respectful and patient, whatever someone's background, identity or experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about work, not people.
- Don't harass, insult, demean or intimidate anyone, publicly or privately. Sexualised language or imagery, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information without permission are not acceptable.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban participants, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@bitop.dev**. Reports are handled in confidence, and we aim to reply within 5 business days. Maintainers who are party to a report take no part in handling it.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct issues: report them as described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
